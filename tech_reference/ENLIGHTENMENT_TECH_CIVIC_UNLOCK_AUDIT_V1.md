## Scientific Theory

Civilization V gives Scientific Theory:
- Public School;
- +1 Production on Lumber Mills;
- a Great Scientist Academy yield upgrade.

The project removes Great Person tile improvements, so the Academy effect is discarded.

Final:
- **Scientific Theory + The Enlightenment -> Public School**
- **Scientific Theory -> Lumber Mill +1 Production**

The Public School is cross-gated because mass public schooling is an institutional reform as well as a scientific-age building.

Civilization VI's Plantation +1 Food effect is moved to later **Fertilizer**, where it is functionally stronger.

Oxford University remains a Wonder candidate.

## Ballistics

Civilization VI puts Field Cannon at Ballistics, but the adopted Enlightenment Era already gave the project **Field Gun at Fortification**.

Civilization V's Machine Gun and Anti-Aircraft Gun are far too late for an 18C Enlightenment Ballistics technology.

Therefore Ballistics does not create a duplicate unit.

Final:
**Ballistics -> Applied Ballistics**
and an upgrade to the existing Field Gun's accuracy/range/efficiency, with exact numbers deferred.

Cuirassier is moved to Military Science because cavalry organization is a stronger fit than projectile science.

## Military Science

Both Civilization V and Civilization VI associate Military Science with Cavalry and Military Academy.

Final:
- **Military Science + Military Training -> Cavalry**
- **Military Science + Military Training -> Military Academy**
- Cuirassier remains an intermediate cavalry candidate at the same broad military-science stage.

Line Infantry stays at Flintlock because Pouakai Enlightenment Era already places it there and the project's firearm chronology is built around that node.

Medic is moved to later **Sanitation**, where health/medical organization fits better.

## Economics

Civilization VI gives Economics the Stock Exchange.
Civilization V gives Economics the Windmill and upgrades Gold on several tile improvements.

Final:
- **Economics -> Stock Exchange**, requiring Bank.
- **Economics + Mercantilism -> Customs House** as a normal city building.

The Civ5 Great Merchant Customs House **tile improvement remains removed**. The historically real customs-house institution is repurposed as a city/port building instead.

Windmill is remapped to **Machinery** because windmills predate academic Economics by centuries.

Camp/Trading Post Gold effects are dropped because they do not fit the project's improvement system well.

## Chemistry

Civilization V Chemistry gives Cannon and +1 Production to Mines, Quarries and Manufactories.
Civilization VI Chemistry gives Research Lab and AT Crew.

The project's Chemistry is moved to the 18th century, so Civ6's Research Lab and AT Crew are too late.

Final:
- **Chemistry -> Chemical Laboratory**
- **Chemistry -> Mine +1 Production**
- **Chemistry -> Quarry +1 Production**

The Chemical Laboratory is an earlier scientific institution, not the later powered Research Lab.

The Great Engineer Manufactory improvement is absent, so that Civ5 yield effect is not imported.

Cannon is not added because the project already has Bombard and Field Gun stages.

Existing corporation gates remain:
- **Chemistry + Capitalism -> Chemicals & Industrial Materials**
- **Chemistry + Capitalism -> Cosmetics & Personal Care**

## Manufacturing

Pouakai Enlightenment Era gives Manufacturing:
- Coal reveal;
- +2 Production on Manufactories;
and adds the Cloth Mill building.

The project removes Great Engineer Manufactory tiles, so that yield upgrade is discarded.

Final:
- **Manufacturing -> Coal reveal**
- **Manufacturing + Guilds -> Cloth Mill**
- **Manufacturing + Guilds -> Proto-industrial Manufacturing system**

Coal uses the ordinary Mine improvement once revealed.

Manufacturing remains a precursor to Industrialization rather than immediately unlocking a modern Factory.

## The Enlightenment

Civilization VI unlocks:
- Rationalism
- Free Market
- Liberalism

All three are retained, but district-based conditions are converted:

Rationalism:
- science-building conditions based on Library/University/Public School/Research Lab rather than Campus adjacency.

Free Market:
- Market/Bank/Stock Exchange rather than Commercial Hub adjacency.

Liberalism:
- developed-city institutional/building threshold rather than specialty-district count.

The Civ6 reduction in religious-tourism influence is retained conceptually.

## Opera and Ballet

Civilization VI provides:
- Grand Opera
- Symphonies
- Bolshoi Theatre

The wonder is deferred.

Grand Opera and Symphonies are retained but converted to direct city-building logic:
- Opera House
- Musicians' Guild
- later Broadcast Center.

Acoustics provides the technical/venue side; Opera and Ballet provides the mature cultural institution.

## Natural History

Civilization VI directly gives:
- Antiquity Sites;
- Archaeologist;
- Zoo;
- archaeological/museum systems.

The Renaissance audit already moved Archaeological Museum away from Humanism to Natural History.

Final:
- **Natural History -> Antiquity Sites**
- **Natural History -> Archaeological Museum**
- **Natural History -> Archaeologist**
- **Natural History -> Zoo**
- artifact excavation/display system.

Art Museum remains at Humanism.

Pouakai Enlightenment Era's generic Museum is merged into this Art Museum / Archaeological Museum split rather than creating a redundant third museum.

Landmark is handled only as part of the archaeology system, never as a Great Artist tile improvement.

## Constitutionalism

Constitutionalism is a project civic.

Final identity:
- **Constitutional Government**
- Separation of Powers policy candidate
- Bill of Rights policy candidate

It does not automatically add a new government form yet. Existing Monarchy/Republic-type governments may receive constitutional variants during the government-roster audit.

## Public Sphere

Public Sphere is a project civic linked historically to print, debate and public opinion.

Final:
**Printing + Public Sphere -> Newspaper Office**

The Newspaper Office is a normal Civ V-style city building and forms a bridge between the Printing Press and later electronic Mass Media/Broadcast Center.

Public Sphere also provides:
- Public Debate/Public Opinion system;
- Pamphleteering policy;
- Coffeehouse Debate as a policy/system candidate.

A universal Coffeehouse building is deliberately not locked because that would over-generalize a culturally specific institution.
