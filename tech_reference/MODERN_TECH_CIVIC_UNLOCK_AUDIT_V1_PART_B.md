# Modern Technology + Civic Unlock Audit V1 — Part B

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **PART B COMPLETE — Modern era fully covered**

Covered technologies:
Combined Arms, Plastics, Penicillin, Electronics, Radar, Atomic Theory.

Covered civics:
Totalitarianism, Class Struggle, Professional Sports.

## Combined Arms

Civ V places Tank and Anti-Tank Gun at Combined Arms.
Civ VI places Destroyer and Aircraft Carrier there and reveals Uranium.

Final:
- **Combined Arms -> Tank**
- **Combined Arms + Military Science -> Anti-Tank Gun**
- **Combined Arms -> Destroyer**
- **Combined Arms + Mobilization -> Marine**

Aircraft Carrier moves to Electronics under Civ V priority.
Uranium moves to Atomic Theory under Civ V priority.

This preserves:
Combustion -> Landship -> Combined Arms -> Tank.

## Plastics

Civ V:
- Infantry
- Research Lab

Civ VI:
- Spec Ops
- Offshore Oil Rig
- Fishing Boats +1 Food

Final:
- **Plastics -> Research Lab**
- **Plastics -> Offshore Oil Rig**
- Infantry remains Replaceable Parts + Nationalism.
- Spec Ops is deferred to the Radar/Rapid Deployment military line.
- Fishing Boats +1 Food is dropped as weakly related late-game yield stacking.

Hydro Plant remains Electricity + Civil Engineering.

## Penicillin

Civ V Penicillin provides Medical Lab, Marine and an extra Trade Route.

Final:
- **Penicillin + Urbanization -> Medical Lab**
- **Penicillin -> Antibiotic Medicine**
- strong bacterial-disease treatment / Health improvement
- Pharmaceutical corporation upgrade

Marine moves to Combined Arms + Mobilization.
The trade-route bonus is dropped.

This continues the health chain:
Sanitation -> public health -> Biology/Hospital -> Penicillin/Medical Lab.

Penicillin greatly reduces bacterial infection mortality but does not make all epidemics impossible.

## Electronics

Civ V BNW gives Electronics Aircraft Carrier and Battleship.

Final:
- **Electronics -> Aircraft Carrier**
- Electronic Control Systems
- electronics-sector corporation gates

Battleship already belongs to:
**Armor Plating + Warships**.

Corporate gates:
- Batteries & Electrified Mobility
- Consumer Electronics
- Electronics Manufacturing
- Semiconductors
all require Electronics + Capitalism.

Machinery & Precision Components receives its existing Electronics upgrade.

## Radar

Civ V Radar gives Fighter, Bomber, Paratrooper and Airport.

The project's functional split is:
- Fighter/Bomber -> Advanced Flight
- Paratrooper -> Advanced Flight + Mobilization
- **Radar + Mass Media -> Airport**
- **Radar + Mobilization -> Radar Station / Air Detection Network**

Radar therefore has a strong air-control and detection identity instead of duplicating aircraft-design technology.

Spec Ops remains deferred pending the Atomic Rapid Deployment audit.

## Atomic Theory

Civ V reveals Uranium and allows the Manhattan Project at Atomic Theory.

The project places Atomic Theory in the early 20th century, before practical nuclear weapons.

Final:
- **Atomic Theory -> Uranium reveal**
- Uranium uses Mine
- Atomic Physics Framework

Manhattan Project moves to:
**Nuclear Fission + Nuclear Program**

The Great Scientist Academy yield upgrade is removed with the Academy itself.

## Totalitarianism

Final:

- Lightning Warfare
- Martial Law
- Third Alternative
- Police State

District references in Third Alternative are translated directly into Research Lab, Military Academy and Power Plant city-building bonuses.

## Class Struggle

Civ VI directly unlocks Communism.

Final:
- **Class Struggle -> Communism**
- Collectivization
- Defense of the Motherland
- Five-Year Plan

Five-Year Plan is moved from the generic Ideology bucket to this specific government branch.

Campus/Industrial Zone adjacency references are translated into direct science-building and industrial-building bonuses.

## Professional Sports

Civ VI unlocks Stadium, Aquatics Center, Ski Resort and Sports Media here.

All generic content is retained in Civ V-style form:
- **Professional Sports -> Stadium**
- **Professional Sports -> Aquatics Center**
- **Professional Sports -> Ski Resort**
- Sports Media
- Professional Sports League system

Stadium is therefore permanently removed from Civ5 Refrigeration.

Ice Hockey Rink remains Canadian unique content.
Estádio do Maracanã remains a Wonder candidate.
