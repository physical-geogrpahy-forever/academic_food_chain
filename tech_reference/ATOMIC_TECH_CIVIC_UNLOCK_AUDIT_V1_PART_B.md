# Atomic Technology + Civic Unlock Audit V1 — Part B

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **PART B COMPLETE — Atomic era fully covered**

Covered technologies:
Synthetic Materials, Composites, Stealth Technology, Robotics, Nuclear Fusion, Ecology, Particle Physics.

Covered civics:
Rapid Deployment, Space Race, Environmentalism.

## Synthetic Materials

Civilization VI unlocks Helicopter and Geothermal Plant and gives a late Camp Gold bonus.

Final:
- **Synthetic Materials -> Helicopter**
- Synthetic Fibers and Polymers system
- Geothermal Plant moves to Ecology + Environmentalism
- Camp Gold bonus is dropped.

## Composites

Civilization VI unlocks Modern Armor, Modern AT and Wind Farm.

Final:
- **Composites + Mobilization -> Modern Armor**
- **Composites + Military Science -> Modern AT**
- **Composites + Environmentalism -> Wind Farm**

This separates material capability from military/environmental institutions.

## Stealth Technology

Civ5/Civ6 both use stealth technology for a top-tier bomber.

Final:
**Advanced Flight + Stealth Technology -> Stealth Bomber**

Stealth Technology also establishes Low-Observable Design as a general system.

## Robotics

Civilization VI gives Robotics Giant Death Robot and a Mars habitation project.

The project keeps Robotics grounded in late-20C automation:
- Industrial Robotics
- factory/electronics automation
- corporate upgrades to Computing Hardware and Electronics Manufacturing.

Giant Death Robot is retained only as an optional endgame/future military project, not as a normal Atomic baseline unit.

Mars Habitation moves to the later Offworld Mission + Exodus Imperative chain.

## Nuclear Fusion

Civilization VI:
- Operation Ivy
- Build Thermonuclear Device
- Launch Mars Reactor.

Final:
- **Nuclear Fusion + Nuclear Program -> Operation Ivy**
- same cross-gate -> Thermonuclear Device after Operation Ivy
- Launch Mars Reactor moves to the later offworld program.

## Ecology

Civ5 Ecology gives Solar Plant and, in expansions, Recycling Center.

The project also remaps renewable/climate content that was awkwardly attached elsewhere in Civ6.

Final:
- **Ecology + Environmentalism -> Solar Plant**
- **Ecology + Environmentalism -> Recycling Center**
- **Ecology + Environmentalism -> Solar Farm**
- **Ecology + Environmentalism -> Geothermal Plant**
- **Ecology + Environmentalism -> Flood Barrier**
- pollution/ecosystem monitoring

Solar Farm no longer belongs to Satellites.
Geothermal Plant no longer belongs to Synthetic Materials.
Flood Barrier no longer belongs to Computers.

## Particle Physics

Civ5 Particle Physics unlocks the SS Engine.

Final:
- **Particle Physics + Space Race -> SS Engine**
- High-Energy Particle Physics system
- Particle Accelerator retained only as a possible late research institution, not yet locked.

No redundant everyday building is invented simply to fill the node.

## Rapid Deployment

Civilization VI Rapid Deployment enables airlifting between Airport-equipped Aerodromes and unlocks Military First and After Action Reports.

Because the project has no Aerodrome districts:
- **Radar + Rapid Deployment + Airport endpoints -> Global Airlift**
- **Radar + Rapid Deployment -> Spec Ops**
- Military First
- After Action Reports.

## Space Race

Civ6 Spaceport district is removed.

Replacement:
**Rocketry + Space Race -> Space Launch Center**

The Space Launch Center is one-per-civilization city/national infrastructure.

It supports:
- Satellites + Space Race -> orbital network / Moon Landing
- Particle Physics + Space Race -> SS Engine
- later offworld projects.

Policies retained:
- Integrated Space Cell
- Satellite Broadcasts
- Music Censorship.

## Environmentalism

Civ6 Environmentalism gives +25% empire Tourism and is inspired by Solar Farms.

The project retains the concept:
- environmental Tourism bonus;
- renewable-energy transition;
- industrial environmental regulation.

It is also the institutional co-gate for:
- Solar Farm
- Wind Farm
- Geothermal Plant
- Flood Barrier
- Solar Plant / Recycling Center.

## Atomic-era status

Together with Part A, all 13 Atomic technologies and all 6 Atomic civics are now audited.
