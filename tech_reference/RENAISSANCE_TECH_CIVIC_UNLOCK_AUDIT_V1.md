## Banking

Civ V and Civ VI both unlock the Bank at Banking. Civ V also places Constabulary and an extra trade route here; Civ VI adds a Quarry Gold bonus.

Final:
- **Banking -> Bank**
- **Banking + Foreign Trade -> additional trade capacity**
- **Constabulary -> Diplomatic Service**
- Quarry Gold bonus is dropped.

The existing corporation map is imported unchanged:
- **Banking + Capitalism -> Finance & Banking corporations**
- **Banking + Capitalism -> Insurance corporations**

## Printing

The Early Medieval chain is preserved:

Writing -> Written Culture -> Scriptorium -> Block Printing -> Woodblock Printing House -> **Printing -> Printing Press**

Printing itself provides the production technology.

Broader social effects require the corresponding civic:
- **Printing + Print Culture -> Mass Print Culture**
- **Printing + Print Culture -> Great Works of Writing Tourism boost**
- **Printing + Diplomatic Service -> Diplomatic Visibility +1**
- **Printing + Diplomatic Service -> International Congress / World Congress enabling condition**

This retains the important Civ VI and Civ V Printing effects without pretending that a machine alone creates a mass-reading society or professional diplomacy.

Civ V's Zoo-at-Printing placement is rejected and deferred to Natural History.

## Metal Casting

Civ VI places Bombard and Pike and Shot at Metal Casting.

Final:
- **Metal Casting -> Bombard**
- **Metal Casting + Military Training -> Pike and Shot**
- advanced cannon casting system

Civ V's generic Forge and Workshop are not copied here:
- Workshop is already at Apprenticeship.
- a generic Forge is historically much older than Renaissance Metal Casting.

## Siege Tactics

Civ VI gives Renaissance Walls and Fort at Siege Tactics.

The project already has:
- Walls -> Masonry
- Castle -> Castles
- Fort -> Engineering

Therefore:
- **Siege Tactics -> Renaissance/Bastion Walls**, upgrading Castle;
- **Siege Tactics -> Bastion Fort upgrade**, upgrading the existing Fort.

This represents the gunpowder-era fortification response without restoring the removed Great General Citadel improvement.

## Metallurgy

Civ V puts Lancer and Arsenal at Metallurgy.

Both fit and are retained:
- **Metallurgy -> Lancer**
- **Metallurgy + Military Training -> Arsenal**

The earlier Gunpowder audit deliberately refused to place a mature Musketman in Early Medieval.

Final Renaissance firearm progression:
- Gunpowder -> Fire Lance
- Metal Casting -> Bombard
- **Metallurgy -> Arquebusier**
- later Flintlock -> mature flintlock musket infantry

Thus Civ V/Civ VI's generic Musketman-at-Gunpowder placement is not copied because the project's technology chronology is different.

## Humanism

Civ VI Humanism unlocks both Art Museum and Archaeological Museum plus Frescoes and Invention.

Final:
- **Humanism -> Art Museum**
- Frescoes
- Invention

Archaeological Museum is moved to **Natural History**, together with Archaeologist, because archaeological science/public artifact museums fit that later civic better.

The Theater Square district remains removed.

## Diplomatic Service

Civ VI directly provides:
- Chancery
- first Spy
- Resident Embassies
- formal Casus Belli
- Machiavellianism
- Wisselbanken

These are retained in Civ V-style form.

Civ V's Constabulary is moved from Banking to Diplomatic Service because counter-intelligence is an administrative/security institution, not a financial technology.

## Patronage

Patronage is a project Renaissance civic drawing heavily on Civ V's Patronage policy tree.

Final baseline:
- **Great Person Patronage** using Gold/Faith, exact cost later;
- slower city-state influence decay / stronger patron relationships;
- Philanthropy;
- Cultural Diplomacy;
- Merchant Confederacy.

The Civ V finisher concept of allied city-states occasionally gifting Great People is retained only as a later reward candidate, not an automatic civic unlock.

Scholasticism remains its own earlier project civic and is not duplicated here.

## Print Culture

Print Culture is a project civic.

It represents the social adoption of printing:
- wider literacy/readership;
- circulation of books and pamphlets;
- cultural and political use of print.

Final:
**Printing + Print Culture -> Mass Print Culture**

Civ VI's doubled Tourism from Great Works of Writing is attached to this cross-gate rather than to Printing alone.

Vernacular Print and Pamphleteering are recorded as future policy candidates, but no arbitrary numeric effects are locked yet.
