# Information Technology + Civic Unlock Audit V1 — Part A

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **PART A COMPLETE — 6 technologies + 6 civics**

Covered technologies:
Nanotechnology, Advanced AI, Advanced Power Cells, Cybernetics, Smart Materials, Predictive Systems.

Covered civics:
Globalization, Social Media, Near Future Governance, Venture Politics, Distributed Sovereignty, Optimization Imperative.

## Nanotechnology
Civ5 unlocks XCOM Squad and SS Stasis Chamber at Nanotechnology.
Civ6 uses Nanotechnology for Mars Colony/Hydroponics.

Final:
- Nanotechnology + Rapid Deployment -> XCOM Squad
- Nanoscale Manufacturing and Medicine
- Nanotechnology + Exodus Imperative -> SS Stasis Chamber
- Mars Colony/Hydroponics move to Offworld Mission + Exodus Imperative.

## Advanced AI
Civ6 Future Era uses Advanced AI only as a Giant Death Robot anti-air upgrade.

The project broadens it to:
- Advanced AI Decision Systems;
- later Information Warfare intelligence support.

The GDR Drone Air Defense upgrade is recorded only if the optional GDR system is adopted.

## Advanced Power Cells
Civ6 uses this mainly for a GDR siege-cannon upgrade and reduced unit emissions.

The project adds the general civilian role:
- High-density Energy Storage
- Advanced Power Cells + Global Warming Mitigation -> Grid Battery Storage
- cleaner-energy military/logistics concept
- Batteries & Electrified Mobility corporation upgrade.

## Cybernetics
Civ6 provides a GDR mobility upgrade and a Lumber Mill bonus.

Final:
- Human-Machine Interface
- possible assistive/medical cybernetics
- GDR mobility only as optional endgame content
- Lumber Mill bonus dropped as functionally weak.

## Smart Materials
Civ6 gives Exoplanet Expedition and a GDR armor upgrade plus Mine Production.

Final:
- Adaptive Smart Materials
- Mine +1 Production
- GDR armor upgrade optional
- Exoplanet Expedition moves to Offworld Mission + Exodus Imperative.

## Predictive Systems
Civ6 gives Offshore Wind Farm and +1 Production to Quarry/Oil Well/Offshore Oil Rig.

Final:
- Predictive Maintenance and Logistics
- Predictive Systems + Environmentalism -> Offshore Wind Farm
- Predictive Systems + Optimization Imperative -> extraction-efficiency bonus
- possible city-project forecasting synergy.

## Globalization
Civ6 provides:
- Strategic Air Force
- International Space Agency
- Ecommerce.

All three policies are retained.

Telecommunications + Globalization creates the institutional/technical Global Trade Network.

The source Plantation Gold bonus is dropped.

## Social Media
Civ6 provides:
- Online Communities
- Collective Activism
- Communications Office.

All are retained/adapted.

Telecommunications + Social Media -> Networked Public Sphere.

Governor-based effects are not automatically imported because the project has not yet adopted the full Governor system.

## Near Future Governance
Retains:
- late-game governance-capacity role
- +3 Envoys.

Governor Title remains deferred.

This civic is the gateway to the project's three Tier-4 government paths.

## Venture Politics
Directly unlocks:
**Corporate Libertarianism**

Civ6 government effects are translated away from districts:
- Commercial Hub -> Market/Bank/Stock Exchange group
- Encampment -> Barracks/Armory/Military Academy group.

Resource accumulation concept is retained.
Exact science penalty is deferred.

## Distributed Sovereignty
Directly unlocks:
**Digital Democracy**

District-based Culture bonuses are converted to direct institutional-city bonuses.

The source military-strength penalty remains a government-balance candidate rather than being locked here.

## Optimization Imperative
Directly unlocks:
**Synthetic Technocracy**

Project translation:
- Advanced Power Cells + Optimization Imperative -> city Power bonus
- Predictive Systems + Optimization Imperative -> city-project production bonus.

Exact source values and Tourism penalty remain for government balance.
