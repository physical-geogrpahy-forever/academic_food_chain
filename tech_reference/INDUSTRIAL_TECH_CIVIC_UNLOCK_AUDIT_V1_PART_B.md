# Industrial Technology + Civic Unlock Audit V1 — Part B

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **PART B COMPLETE — Industrial era now covered in two parts**

## Technologies covered

Mass Production, Fertilizer, Biology, Dynamite, Railroad, Refrigeration, Telegraph, Armor Plating.

## Civics covered

Conservation, Mass Media, Capitalism, Romanticism, Labor Movement.

## Key decisions

### Mass Production
Because the project moved Mass Production to the 19th century, it represents standardized industrial/assembly production rather than Renaissance Venetian ship prefabrication alone.

- Mass Production -> Shipyard
- Assembly-Line Production
- corporation gates: Automotive, Furniture/Household, Retail/Distribution, Textiles/Apparel/Sporting with Capitalism.

Venetian Arsenal remains a wonder candidate.

### Fertilizer
Civ5 agricultural logic is retained selectively:
- non-fresh-water Farm +1 Food;
- Plantation +1 Food.
- Pasture +1 Food remains provisional because Horse Collar and Replaceable Parts already affect Pastures.

### Biology and Health
- Biology + Urbanization -> Hospital.
- Biology provides Medical Biology / Health improvement.
- Pharmaceuticals corporations require Biology + Capitalism.
- Civ5 Oil reveal/Oil Well are moved to Refining.

This creates the health chain:
Sanitation -> Sewer/public health -> Biology -> Hospital -> later Penicillin.

### Dynamite
- Dynamite + Military Science -> Artillery.
- High-Explosive Mining system.
- Mining & Extractive Materials corporations require Dynamite + Capitalism.

### Railroad
- dedicated Railroad technology owns Railroad route construction.
- rail-connected cities gain a production/logistics bonus concept.
- Railroad + Capitalism can expand trade capacity.
- Hospitality/Hotels corporation gate is retained.
- Mining and Shipping/Logistics sectors receive their recorded Railroad upgrades.

Hotel is moved from Civ5 Refrigeration to **Railroad + Capitalism** because rail tourism/commercial hospitality is the stronger functional relationship.

### Refrigeration
- Refrigeration -> Cold Storage.
- Refrigeration + Capitalism -> Refrigerated Food Logistics.
- Processed Food and Food Service corporation gates retained.
- Stadium moves to Professional Sports.
- Offshore Platform moves to Refining.
- Submarine remains associated with Electricity pending final naval-unit reconciliation.

### Telegraph
- Telegraph Network.
- Telegraph + Sovereignty -> Telegraph Office.
- Telecommunications corporation gate retained.
- Battleship and Military Base are removed from Civ5's Telegraph placement because their functions are naval armor and military organization, not telegraphy.

### Armor Plating
Pouakai Enlightenment Era is followed directly:
- Armor Plating -> Cruiser.
- Armor Plating -> Drydock.
- Armor Plating + Warships -> Battleship.
- naval armor modernization.

### Conservation
Civ6 framework retained:
- Naturalist;
- National Parks;
- Worker reforestation;
- old-growth Woods appeal;
- Resource Management.
Preserve district is removed; Sanctuary may survive as a direct conservation city building.

### Mass Media
Mass Media does not prematurely unlock electronic broadcasting:
- Telegraph + Mass Media -> mass-circulation media system.
- Propaganda policy retained.
- **Radio + Mass Media -> Broadcast Center** later.

### Capitalism
Capitalism is the global civic gate for the corporation system.

It unlocks:
- corporate founding permission;
- investment/branch expansion;
- Laissez-Faire;
- Market Economy;
- Mass Production + Capitalism -> Shopping Mall.

A corporation still also needs its own sector technology.

### Romanticism
Pouakai EE Romanticism content is reconciled rather than copied:
- Gallery merges with Art Museum.
- Menagerie merges with Zoo.
- Surveyor merges with Naturalist/survey systems.
- Romantic Landscape Aesthetics becomes the civic's core Culture/Tourism role.
- Crystal Palace remains a Wonder candidate.

### Labor Movement
Project civic baseline:
- Organized Labor Movement.
- Collective Bargaining.
- Factory Acts.

A universal Labor Union Hall is not locked yet because institutional forms vary strongly among societies.
Strike/labor-dispute mechanics are deferred until the economy simulation exists.
