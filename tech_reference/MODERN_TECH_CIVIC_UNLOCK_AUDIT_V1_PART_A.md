# Modern Technology + Civic Unlock Audit V1 — Part A

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **PART A COMPLETE — 6 technologies + 3 civics**

Covered technologies:
Flight, Radio, Combustion, Advanced Flight, Rocketry, Advanced Ballistics.

Covered civics:
Mobilization, Ideology, Suffrage.

## Aviation

Flight:
- Triplane
- Great War Bomber
- Airstrip
- Hangar
- Observation Balloon with Mobilization
- Aerodrome district removed

Advanced Flight:
- Fighter
- Bomber
- Paratrooper with Mobilization
- corporation upgrades for aerospace, airlines, hospitality and logistics.

Civ V places Fighter/Bomber/Airport later at Radar, while Civ6 places Fighter/Bomber/Airport at Advanced Flight.
The project uses functional separation:
- aircraft design -> Advanced Flight;
- controlled mass-airport/radar infrastructure -> Radar.

## Radio

Final:
- Radio + Mass Media -> Broadcast Center
- Radio + Mass Media -> Film Studio
- Film/Media corporation sector upgrade

Civ6's Aluminum reveal at Radio is rejected.
Industrial aluminum production depends on electrical electrolysis, so:
**Electricity -> Aluminum reveal**
is the cross-era correction.

Seaside Resort is moved to:
**Advanced Flight + Mass Media**
because mass tourism is a stronger fit than radio alone.

## Combustion

Civ V priority is retained:
**Combustion -> Landship**

Civ6's Supply Convoy becomes:
**Combustion + Mobilization -> Supply Convoy**

Mature Tank moves to Combined Arms, preserving the Civ5 Landship -> Tank progression.

Motorized trade/logistics requires Capitalism for the commercial institutional side.

## Rocketry

The project places Rocketry in the early 20th century.

Therefore it does NOT immediately unlock:
- Spaceport;
- Apollo Program;
- Earth satellite launch;
- strategic Missile Silo.

Final:
**Rocketry + Military Science -> Rocket Artillery**

Later:
- Mobile SAM -> Guidance Systems
- Missile Silo -> Guidance Systems + Nuclear Program
- orbital program -> Satellites + Space Race

## Advanced Ballistics

Final:
- Machine Gun
- Anti-Air Gun

Civ6 places both here and they fit the project's early/mid-20C military chronology.

Guided Missile, Nuclear Missile and spaceship booster are remapped later because they require guidance, nuclear and space technologies rather than ballistics alone.

## Mobilization

Civ6 structure retained:
- Armies and Armadas
- Levée en Masse
- Territorial Expansion War

Cross-gates:
- Advanced Flight + Mobilization -> Paratrooper
- Combustion + Mobilization -> Supply Convoy

## Ideology

Ideology becomes the shared modern ideological-policy layer:
- Five-Year Plan
- Gunboat Diplomacy
- Lightning Warfare
- Economic Union
- Police State
- Ideological War
- additional Spy capacity

Final policy ownership may narrow when the three Tier-3 governments are resolved:
Democracy, Fascism and Communism.

Tier-3 Government Plaza buildings are deferred until all three branches are audited together.

## Suffrage

Final:
- Democracy
- expanded political participation
- New Deal
- Economic Union
- Their Finest Hour
- Arsenal of Democracy concept

Civ6 specialty-district requirements are translated into normal Civ V-style urban-building/infrastructure thresholds.
