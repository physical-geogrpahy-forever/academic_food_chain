## Major reconciliations

### Castles
Civ V places Castle at Chivalry. Civ VI has a dedicated Castles technology that unlocks Medieval Walls.

Final:
**Castles -> Castle**

Civ5 Castle and Civ6 Medieval Walls are merged into one Civ V-style defensive city building requiring Walls.

The Civ6 Courser is not retained at Castles. Cavalry development belongs with Stirrups/mounted-unit progression.

### Gunpowder
Civ V and Civ VI both associate Gunpowder with Musketman.

The project deliberately moved Gunpowder to Early Medieval because the historical gunpowder tradition appears centuries before mature muskets.

Final:
- **Gunpowder -> Fire Lance**
- **Gunpowder -> Gunpowder Munitions**
- previously revealed **Niter** becomes a usable military input.

Musketman is **not** unlocked here. It is provisionally remapped to the later Metallurgy/firearms stage and must be confirmed during Renaissance audit.

Civ6 Quarry +1 Production is dropped because quarry blasting is not an appropriate representation of early medieval gunpowder.

### Block Printing
The project added Block Printing as an Early Medieval historical technology.

Woodblock printing appeared in China around the Tang period and enabled repeated publication of religious, agricultural, medical, calendar, administrative and commercial texts.

Final:
**Written Culture -> Scriptorium**
then
**Block Printing + Written Culture -> Woodblock Printing House**

This creates a clean chain:
Writing -> manuscript culture -> block printing -> later Printing.

The later Renaissance **Printing** technology retains the mechanized/movable-type printing-press stage and its wider diplomatic/mass-cultural effects.

### Algebra
Algebra remains primarily an abstract mathematical capability.

Final:
**Algebra -> Advanced Algebraic Calculation**

No generic building is invented merely to fill the technology.

Possible later effects:
- stronger Library/Scriptorium/Paper Workshop science;
- administration and commercial calculation;
- support for Physics/Astronomy.

Exact yields remain deferred.

### Astrolabe
Astrolabe represents medieval practical celestial observation and navigation.

Final:
- improved naval positional information;
- improved maritime sight/trade range;
- possible Harbor/Lighthouse navigation synergy.

It does **not** unlock ocean crossing.

Civ V's ocean-crossing milestone remains later at Astronomy.

## Feudalism
Civ VI Feudalism supplies Feudal Contract, Serfdom and a Farm-clustering bonus.

The project keeps that institutional identity.

Because the project uses Civ V Workers, not expendable Civ VI Builders:
- Serfdom cannot grant extra Builder charges;
- candidate adaptation is faster Worker improvement construction.

The Civ VI Farm adjacency concept becomes **Feudal Field System**, with exact formula deferred until farm-yield stacking is tested against Irrigation, Calendar and Horse Collar.

A generic Estate/Manor building is not yet locked because that would risk treating a specifically European form as universally mandatory.

## Mercenaries
Civ VI Mercenaries directly provides:
- Professional Army
- Retinues
- Sack
- Trade Confederation

These are retained as policy candidates.

A broader Gold-based Mercenary Recruitment system is historically attractive but is deferred until the unit/economy system can define contracts, duration and upgrade behavior.

The Jong remains an Indonesian unique unit.

## Written Culture
Written Culture is a project-added civic, so it needs its own institutional content.

Final:
**Writing + Written Culture -> Scriptorium**

Scriptorium:
- manuscript copying/preservation institution;
- may hold a Great Work of Writing slot;
- supports written culture without replacing Writers' Guild as the Great Writer generation building.

Block Printing later upgrades this institutional chain into the Woodblock Printing House.

## Court Culture
Court Culture is also a project-added civic.

Final:
**Court Culture -> Court**

The Court is:
- a Palace-linked city building;
- one per civilization;
- focused on elite cultural patronage and diplomatic display.

Potential later effects include Great Writer/Artist support and additional Palace Great Work capacity, but exact numbers are intentionally not invented in this pass.
