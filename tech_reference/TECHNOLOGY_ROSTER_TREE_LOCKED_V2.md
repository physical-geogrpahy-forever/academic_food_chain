## Why the 103-node V1 was revised

The source-only merge left only two technologies in both Late Antiquity and Early Medieval. It also retained game-era placements that are difficult to defend historically:

- Stirrups were placed in the project's 7-10C Early Medieval era even though firm East Asian archaeological evidence exists by the 4C and paired stirrups were widespread in East Asia by the 5C.
- Gunpowder remained in the 14-15C Renaissance even though Chinese gunpowder formula traditions are attested in the 9C and military applications followed in later centuries.

V2 therefore makes a **minimal historical correction**, rather than filling eras arbitrarily.

## Historical rationale

Papermaking:
- paper existed in ancient China and papermaking spread through Central Asia and the Islamic world by the 8-10C.

Block Printing:
- woodblock printing appeared in China around 600 and became established under Tang rule.

Algebra:
- al-Khwarizmi's ninth-century work is a foundational milestone for the named algebraic tradition.

Astrolabe:
- Greek antecedents were substantially developed and widely used in the medieval Islamic world; Arabic astrolabe traditions are documented from the 8-10C.

Stirrups:
- firm archaeological evidence exists in 4C East Asia; paired stirrups were widespread by the 5C and spread further west thereafter.

Horse Collar:
- a rigid padded collar is represented in 5C Chinese evidence and later spread westward.

Gunpowder:
- Chinese textual evidence for gunpowder formulations dates to the 9C; gunpowder weapons developed thereafter.
