# Atomic Technology + Civic Unlock Audit V1 — Part A

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **PART A COMPLETE — 6 technologies + 3 civics**

Covered technologies:
Computers, Nuclear Fission, Telecommunications, Satellites, Guidance Systems, Lasers.

Synthetic Materials is intentionally left for Part B with the remaining Atomic material/ecology technologies.

Covered civics:
Nuclear Program, Cultural Heritage, Cold War.

## Computers

Civilization VI Computers unlocks Drone and Flood Barrier, adds Spy capacity and increases Tourism.

Final:
- Computers + Rapid Deployment -> Drone
- Digital Information Processing system
- Computers + Cold War -> one merged additional-Spy milestone
- Computers + Mass Media -> digital Tourism/information-network bonus
- Flood Barrier moves to Ecology + Environmentalism

Corporation integration:
- Computing Hardware and Software & IT Services founding with Capitalism
- digital upgrades to Consumer Electronics, Digital Platforms, Finance, Insurance and Semiconductors.

## Nuclear Fission

Civilization VI unlocks Nuclear Power Plant, Manhattan Project and Nuclear Device production here.

Final:
- Nuclear Fission + Civil Engineering -> Nuclear Power Plant
- Nuclear Fission + Nuclear Program -> Manhattan Project
- same cross-gate + completed Manhattan Project -> Build Nuclear Device
- Uranium was already revealed by Atomic Theory.

This preserves the Modern decision not to weaponize Atomic Theory itself.

## Telecommunications

Civilization VI only gives Nuclear Submarine to Telecommunications.

That placement is rejected as functionally weak.

Final:
- Telecommunications -> Global Telecommunications Network
- Telecommunications + Capitalism -> Digital Platforms & E-commerce sector
- Telecommunications -> Telecommunications sector upgrade
- Nuclear Submarine -> Nuclear Fission + Cold War

## Satellites

Civilization VI Satellites provides Solar Farm, Mechanized Infantry and Moon Landing.

Final:
- Satellites + Space Race -> Orbital Satellite Network
- Satellites + Space Race -> Launch Moon Landing
- Satellites -> Global Mapping / Remote Sensing
- Mechanized Infantry moves to Combined Arms.
- Solar Farm moves to Ecology + Environmentalism.

Satellite Broadcasts remains a Space Race policy, matching Civilization VI.

## Guidance Systems

Civilization VI unlocks Mobile SAM and Rocket Artillery here.

The project already unlocked Rocket Artillery at Modern Rocketry.

Final:
- Guidance Systems + Mobilization -> Mobile SAM
- Guidance Systems -> Guided Missile
- Guidance Systems -> precision upgrade for Rocket Artillery
- Guidance Systems + Nuclear Program -> Missile Silo

This makes Guidance Systems a precision-control node instead of duplicating Rocketry.

## Lasers

Civilization VI unlocks Jet Fighter and Missile Cruiser here.

Final:
- Advanced Flight + Lasers -> Jet Fighter
- Lasers -> Laser Systems and advanced targeting
- Missile Cruiser moves to Guidance Systems + Warships

The project therefore does not pretend that the cruiser itself is primarily a laser technology.

## Nuclear Program

Civilization VI Nuclear Program provides Science Foundations and Nuclear Espionage.

Both are retained.

The civic is also the state-program side of:
- Manhattan Project
- nuclear weapons production
- Missile Silo infrastructure

## Cultural Heritage

Civilization VI:
- reveals Shipwrecks;
- enables Heritage Tourism;
- awards 3 Envoys.

All are retained.

Cultural Heritage extends Conservation:
natural heritage -> cultural/archaeological heritage.

Bonuses to civilization-specific Missions and Cahokia Mounds remain unique.

## Cold War

Civilization VI Cold War provides:
- additional Spy capacity;
- Cryptography;
- Containment;
- International Waters;
- Second Strike Capability;
- Rock Bands in Gathering Storm.

All useful generic concepts are retained.

The extra Spy effect is merged with Computers to avoid duplicate stacking.

Nuclear Submarine becomes:
**Nuclear Fission + Cold War**

Second Strike Capability is meaningful only if nuclear technology exists.
