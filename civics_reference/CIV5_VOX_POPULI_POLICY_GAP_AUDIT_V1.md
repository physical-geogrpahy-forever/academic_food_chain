# Civ V Vox Populi policy gap audit V1

Date: 2026-09-20

Purpose: test whether Vox Populi's policy trees provide additional civic nodes for the sparse project eras after applying the locked Civ VI-priority rule.

## Medieval policy trees checked

Vox Populi unlocks Fealty, Statecraft and Artistry in the Medieval Era.

### Fealty

Important policy names include:
- Divine Right
- Fiefdoms / Fiefs
- Nobility
- Organized Religion
- Serfdom

Civ VI-priority result:
- Divine Right -> exact Civ VI civic already exists.
- Fiefdoms / Fiefs -> contained by Civ VI Feudalism.
- Nobility -> contained by Feudalism / Divine Right and better reused as policy-card/effect content.
- Organized Religion -> contained by Civ VI Theology.
- Serfdom -> contained by Civ VI Feudalism and its agrarian/worker-policy content.

Result: **no strong new civic candidate**.

### Statecraft

Statecraft focuses on:
- city-state diplomacy
- diplomatic missions
- trade routes
- spies
- World Congress influence

Civ VI-priority result:
- core institutional function is already covered by Diplomatic Service plus existing diplomacy/envoy/spy policy content.

Result: **do not add Statecraft as a separate civic unless a later diplomacy mechanic requires a dedicated node**.

### Artistry

Artistry includes Humanism, Heritage, Cultural Exchange and related great-work/great-person effects.

Civ VI-priority result:
- Humanism is an exact Civ VI civic.
- Heritage is covered by later Cultural Heritage / conservation-museum content.
- Cultural Exchange is better policy-card or tourism content.

Result: **no strong new civic candidate**.

## Conclusion

Vox Populi confirms that the apparent Early Medieval gap should not be solved by duplicating feudal and religious concepts already present in Civ VI.

Strong candidates remain unchanged:
- Scholasticism
- Scientific Revolution
- Sovereignty
- Romanticism

No additional strong candidate is added from Vox Populi at this stage.
