# Exploration Policy Effects V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **LOCKED**

Direct values:
- Colonial Taxes: +25% Gold in overseas-continent cities
- Native Conquest: earlier-era combat victory Gold = 50% of defeated unit Combat Strength
- Raj: +2 Science/Culture/Faith/Gold per allied/suzerain city-state
- Maritime Infrastructure: coastal cities +3 Production
- Logistics: +1 Movement when starting in friendly territory
- Triangular Trade: every Trade Route +4 Gold, +1 Faith
- Religious Orders: +5 Religious Strength
- Wars of Religion: +4 Combat Strength against civilizations following another religion

Structural adaptation:
- Simultaneum: Shrine and Temple base Faith output doubled.

The Worker-built Holy Site tile is not doubled by Simultaneum because the source card applies to religious buildings, not the Holy Site terrain/district itself.
