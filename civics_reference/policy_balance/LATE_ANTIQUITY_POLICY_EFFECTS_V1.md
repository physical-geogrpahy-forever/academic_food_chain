# Late Antiquity Policy Effects V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **LOCKED**

## Directly retained from Civ6
- Bastions: +6 City Defense Strength, +5 City Ranged Strength
- Limes: +100% Production toward defensive buildings
- Navigation: +2 Great Admiral Points/turn
- Retainers: +1 Amenity in cities with a garrisoned unit

## Structural translations

### Scripture
Civ6 doubles Holy Site adjacency.

Project:
**Shrine, Temple, and the Worker-built Holy Site tile each provide +1 Faith.**

### Naval Infrastructure
Civ6 doubles Harbor adjacency.

Project:
**Gold output from Harbor, Lighthouse, and Seaport buildings +50%.**

### Meritocracy
Vanilla Civ6 gives +1 Culture per specialty district; Rise and Fall / Gathering Storm remove the card.

The project retains it because Civil Service benefits from an administrative-merit policy.

Project:
**+1 Culture per distinct institutional building chain in a city, maximum +4 Culture per city.**

The same chain-count definition used for Insulae applies.
