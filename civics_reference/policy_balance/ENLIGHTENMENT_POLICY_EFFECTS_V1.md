# Enlightenment Policy Effects V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **LOCKED**

Civ6 district-output cards are deliberately reduced from +100% to +50% when translated to direct city-building yields, because the project has no district-adjacency layer and multiple later building upgrades can stack.

Final:
- Rationalism: University/Public School/Research Lab Science +50%
- Free Market: Market/Bank/Stock Exchange Gold +50%
- Liberalism: +1 Amenity in cities with at least 2 institutional chains
- Grand Opera: Amphitheater/Opera House/Museum/Broadcast Center Culture +50%
- Symphonies: +4 Great Musician Points/turn
- Separation of Powers: Courthouse and government-administration buildings +1 Culture/+1 Gold
- Bill of Rights: Courthouse cities +1 Amenity, occupation/unrest pressure -25%
- Coffeehouse Debate: University/Opera House/Newspaper Office +1 Science/+1 Culture
- Pamphleteering: Newspaper Office +1 Culture; international Trade Routes +1 Culture
