# High Medieval Policy Effects V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **LOCKED**

Direct Civ6 values retained:
- Chivalry: +50% Production toward Industrial-era and earlier cavalry
- Gothic Architecture: +15% Production toward Ancient-Renaissance Wonders
- Traveling Merchants: +2 Great Merchant Points/turn
- Merchant Confederation: +1 Gold per assigned Envoy

District translations:
- Craftsmen: Workshop, Factory, Power Plant each +1 Production
- Town Charters: Market, Bank, Stock Exchange each +1 Gold
- Aesthetics: each cultural building +1 Culture
- Medina Quarter: +2 Housing in cities with at least 3 institutional building chains

The institutional-chain definition is shared with Insulae and Meritocracy.
