# Information Policy Effects V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **LOCKED**

## Direct Civ6 / Gathering Storm effects retained

- Hallyu: Rock Bands choose any Promotion.
- Non-State Actors: Spies choose any Promotion.
- Space Tourism: incoming foreign Tourism pressure -20%.
- Ecommerce: all Trade Routes +2 Production and +5 Gold.
- International Space Agency: +5% Science per allied/suzerain city-state.
- Strategic Air Force: +50% Production toward all air units and Aircraft Carriers.
- Diplomatic Capital: +4 Diplomatic Favor/turn.
- Global Coalition: +7 Combat Strength in friendly territory.
- Collective Activism: +5% Culture per allied/suzerain city-state.
- Online Communities: +50% Tourism toward civilizations connected by your Trade Route.

## Structural adaptations

### Aerospace Contractors
Civ6 Spaceport is replaced by the project's Space Launch Center.

Final:
**each city with a Space Launch Center gains +3 Aluminum/turn and +3 Power.**

### Integrated Attack Logistics
Baseline:
**+1 Movement to units that start in enemy territory.**

Civ6's +50% Giant Death Robot Production is omitted from the baseline because GDR is optional.
The optional GDR module may restore that clause.

### Rabblerousing
The project does not currently use Civ6 Diplomatic Victory points.

Final baseline:
**+1 Diplomatic Favor/turn.**

The 50% Favor refund tied specifically to reducing another player's Diplomatic Victory points is inactive unless that victory system is later adopted.

### Communications Office
Civ6 makes Governors generate Loyalty by promotion level. Governors are not part of the baseline.

Final:
- Newspaper Office +1 Culture
- Broadcast Center +1 Culture
- a city containing either building suffers 25% less occupation/unrest pressure

This preserves the policy's public-communications and legitimacy role without reintroducing Governors.
