# Early Medieval Policy Effects V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **LOCKED**

## Direct Civ6 values
- Feudal Contract: +50% Production toward Ancient through Renaissance melee, ranged and anti-cavalry units
- Professional Army: unit-upgrade Gold cost -50%
- Retinues: unit-upgrade strategic-resource cost -50%
- Trade Confederation: international Trade Routes +1 Science and +1 Culture

## Worker adaptation

### Serfdom
Civ6 gives newly trained Builders two extra charges.

Workers in this project are Civ V-style persistent units.

Final:
**Workers construct tile improvements 25% faster.**

This applies to ordinary improvements and the restored nerfed former-Great-Person improvements unless a later improvement has its own special construction rule.

## Pillage adaptation

### Sack
Civ6 doubles district-pillage yields.

The project has no districts.

Final:
**Pillage yields from city-linked infrastructure, roads, forts and port infrastructure +100%.**

Ordinary resource/farm/mine pillaging remains governed by Raid/Total War rather than receiving two overlapping doubling effects.

## Project policy

### Manuscript Patronage
- Scriptorium +1 Culture
- Woodblock Printing House +1 Culture
- +1 Great Writer Point per turn
