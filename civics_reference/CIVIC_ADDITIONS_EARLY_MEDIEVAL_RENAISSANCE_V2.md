# Civic additions after Civ VI / Civ V review — V2

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: reviewed additions and one pending Early Medieval candidate

## Adopted additions

The following three civics are now accepted for the project:

### Written Culture
Project era: Early Medieval (7th-10th centuries)

Purpose:
- represents the expansion of written documentation, archives, legal records, administrative writing, manuscript culture and organized written knowledge
- fills a role not directly represented by Civ VI Civil Service or Recorded History alone

Suggested graph role:
Civil Service -> Written Culture -> Scholasticism

Do not use this civic as a replacement for Recorded History or Civil Service; it is an inserted development between late-antique bureaucratic literacy and high-medieval scholarly institutions.

### Patronage
Project era: Renaissance (14th-15th centuries)

Purpose:
- organized elite/court/urban support of artists, scholars and cultural production
- reuses the Civilization V Patronage concept without duplicating any Civilization VI civic name

Suggested graph role:
Court Culture + Humanism -> Patronage

Patronage should unlock cultural/Great Person policies and institutions rather than replace Humanism.

### Print Culture
Project era: Renaissance (14th-15th centuries)

Purpose:
- separates Printing Press as a science/technology node from the social consequences of widespread print
- represents expansion of book circulation, literacy networks, scholarly/religious debate and cheaper textual reproduction

Suggested graph role:
Humanism -> Print Culture -> Scientific Revolution / Reformed Church

The exact two outgoing edges should be graph-tested later.

## Early Medieval re-audit

Existing Civ VI civics assigned to Early Medieval:

- Mercenaries

Adopted addition:

### Strongest additional candidate: Court Culture

Status: recommended, not yet locked until user approval.

Why it survives Civ VI precedence:
- no Civilization VI civic named Court Culture
- it is not equivalent to Feudalism, Civil Service, Theology, Divine Right, Humanism or Patronage
- Civ VI's own Wu Zetian historical entry explicitly describes Tang as an era of court culture
- comparable court-centered political/cultural systems are well documented in Carolingian, Abbasid, Byzantine and Heian contexts

Proposed scope:
- royal/imperial household as a political, administrative and cultural center
- court ceremony and hierarchy
- ruler-centered elite networks
- court sponsorship of literature, scholarship and arts
- connection between administration and elite culture

Suggested graph:
Civil Service + Written Culture -> Court Culture
Court Culture + Humanism -> Patronage

This gives Court Culture a distinct function:
- Civil Service = administration
- Written Culture = documentary/literate social infrastructure
- Court Culture = ruler-centered elite political/cultural institution
- Patronage = later organized support of arts and scholarship

### Secondary candidate: Translation Movement

Status: hold as content/policy rather than civic for now.

Evidence:
- the Graeco-Arabic translation movement in Baghdad is conventionally dated to the 8th-10th centuries
- translation and textual transmission also mattered in Tang China and Carolingian intellectual networks

Reason not to promote yet:
- it is much narrower than Court Culture
- much of its gameplay role can sit inside Written Culture and later Scholasticism/Scientific Revolution

Potential reuse:
- policy card
- Inspiration for Written Culture or Scholasticism
- library/academy project

### Secondary candidate: Jurisprudence

Status: reject as a separate civic for now.

Evidence:
- Islamic judicial/legal theory matured strongly during the 8th-10th centuries
- western canon law also developed substantially during the early Middle Ages

Reason rejected:
- too much functional overlap with Code of Laws + Theology + Civil Service under the locked Civ VI-priority rule

Use instead:
- policy/unlock content attached to Theology, Civil Service or Written Culture

### Rejected duplicate candidates

Manorialism:
- Civ VI Feudalism already unlocks Serfdom and Feudal Contract and carries agrarian/landed relations
- do not create a duplicate civic

Vassalage:
- included in Feudalism / Feudal Contract
- do not add

Monasticism:
- already exists in Civilization VI as a Dark Age policy card
- Theology also carries organized religious institutions
- do not add as a civic

Organized Religion:
- Theology already covers this space
- do not add

Pilgrimage:
- exists in Civilization VI as a religious belief and is better handled by religion mechanics
- do not add as a civic

Vernacular Culture:
- historically plausible, especially 8th-10th century language change and Heian kana
- currently treat as content inside Written Culture rather than a separate node to avoid over-fragmentation
