## 1. Class identity

`Great Revolutionary / 위대한 혁명가` represents people who converted political or social discontent into organized movement, independence struggle, regime change or major social transformation.

Class boundary:
- Great Philosopher: forms or systematizes ideas.
- Great Revolutionary: organizes, mobilizes or leads actual political transformation.
- Great General: primarily commands military forces.

A person may have several historical roles, but the project assigns one primary Great Person class. Philosophers who strongly influenced revolutions normally remain Great Philosophers and interact with GReP instead of being duplicated.

## 2. Great Revolutionary Point

Currency: `GReP`.

GReP is held by a political unit, not by a city specialist.

Political units that may own a separate GReP pool:
- sovereign civilization;
- colonial government;
- autonomous dominion or subject political unit when represented separately by the political system.

A colonial government's GReP is separate from the metropole's GReP.

GReP is NOT generated by a generic Revolutionary specialist.
GReP is NOT awarded simply because Happiness is low.

Core rule:

```text
political organization / ideology
+
grievance or political crisis
+
major political events
-> GReP
```

This prevents deliberate city mismanagement from becoming an optimal Great Revolutionary farming strategy.

## 3. Movement Capacity gate

Recurring political-pressure GReP requires `MOVEMENT_CAPACITY = TRUE`.

Movement Capacity becomes true when at least one qualifying organization source is active in the political unit:
- a civic or policy representing nationalism, self-determination, suffrage, labor organization, revolutionary constitutionalism or anti-colonial organization;
- a political institution explicitly tagged as an organizing institution;
- a colonial self-government institution that permits organized political mobilization;
- a Great Philosopher or other unique effect explicitly tagged `REVOLUTIONARY_THOUGHT` or `POLITICAL_ORGANIZATION`.

Low Happiness, War Weariness or foreign rule alone do not create Movement Capacity.

Major one-shot historical/political events may grant GReP without Movement Capacity when specified in the event table.

## 4. Recurring structural-pressure tick

Evaluation interval on Standard speed: every 5 turns.

A structural-pressure tick occurs only when:
1. Movement Capacity is true; and
2. at least one grievance flag is active.

Count active grievance flags, with severe conditions allowed to count as a second flag:
- average City Happiness <= -1;
- average City Happiness <= -3, additional severe flag;
- empire War Weariness >= 50;
- empire War Weariness >= 80, additional severe flag;
- sustained occupation burden;
- sustained colonial burden;
- autonomy request denied within the recent grievance window;
- State Stability crisis flag, once the State Stability quantitative layer is implemented.

Tick value:

```text
1 grievance      -> +4 GReP
2 grievances     -> +6 GReP
3 or more        -> +8 GReP
```

Maximum recurring structural-pressure gain is therefore +8 GReP per 5 turns before unique-person modifiers.

The severe Happiness and severe War Weariness flags may stack with their lower-threshold flag, but the 3+ grievance cap prevents runaway multiplication.

## 5. Discrete event GReP

The authoritative event values are in:
`city_system/great_revolutionaries/GREP_EVENT_VALUES_V1.csv`.

Key principles:
- major ideological or political milestones are generally +8 to +12;
- severe military/political shocks are generally +5 to +12;
- recurring colonial/occupation pressure is generally +6 with cooldown;
- Great Philosopher effects may grant person-specific GReP, normally +5 to +20;
- ordinary government switching does not grant GReP;
- deliberately switching governments repeatedly cannot farm GReP.

## 6. Pool cap and recruitment threshold

Base recruitment threshold on Standard speed:

```text
First Great Revolutionary in a political unit: 100 GReP
Second: 120 GReP
Third: 140 GReP
Fourth: 160 GReP
Fifth and later: 180 GReP
```

Formula:

```text
threshold = min(180, 100 + 20 * previously_recruited_revolutionaries)
```

When a Great Revolutionary is recruited:
- subtract the current threshold from the political unit's GReP;
- retain overflow;
- increase that political unit's previously-recruited count by 1.

Pool cap:

```text
GReP cap = 2 * current recruitment threshold
```

This prevents unlimited stockpiling while allowing meaningful overflow.

A political unit may have at most one active, unresolved Great Revolutionary at a time. GReP may continue accumulating while one is active, up to the cap, but a second candidate cannot be recruited until the current Revolutionary is consumed, retires or otherwise resolves.

A 15-turn recruitment cooldown begins when the current Great Revolutionary resolves. This prevents back-to-back chain revolutions from stored overflow.

When a colonial government becomes independent, its GReP pool, recruitment count, active cooldown and current Revolutionary state carry into the successor state. Independence therefore cannot reset the cost curve.

## 7. Game-speed scaling

Standard speed is the numeric authority for V1.

When global game-speed multipliers are finalized, both:
- GReP event/tick gains; and
- GReP recruitment thresholds

must be scaled by the same Great Person speed factor so that the expected political time to recruitment is preserved.

Do not independently scale only the threshold or only the gains.

## 8. Candidate model

Great Revolutionary does NOT use a single unconditional worldwide queue in the same way as Great Scientist or Great Philosopher.

Instead, recruitment uses a context-filtered global historical pool.

Every candidate must have:
- historical activity era;
- primary Revolutionary tag;
- optional secondary tags;
- optional regional/civilizational affinity;
- candidate-specific ability;
- class-boundary audit status.

Core candidate tags:
- `REGIME_CHANGE`
- `INDEPENDENCE`
- `ANTI_COLONIAL`
- `LABOR`
- `SOCIAL_REVOLUTION`
- `NATIONAL`
- `DEMOCRATIC`
- `MILITARY_REVOLUTION`
- `REVOLUTIONARY_INTERNATIONALISM`

Candidates may have multiple tags.

## 9. Candidate eligibility and scoring

When the political unit reaches its current GReP threshold, build an eligible candidate list from all globally unrecruited Great Revolutionaries.

Hard eligibility:
- candidate is not already recruited anywhere in the world;
- candidate era is current era or an adjacent eligible era unless a unique historical rule overrides this;
- any explicit candidate hard condition is met;
- candidate is not blocked by Great General / Great Philosopher / leader cross-class assignment.

Context score:

```text
+4  dominant political context matches candidate primary tag
+2  each matching secondary tag, maximum +4
+3  regional/civilizational affinity match
+2  exact activity-era match
+1  adjacent-era match
+2  active independence crisis and candidate has INDEPENDENCE or ANTI_COLONIAL
+2  active regime crisis and candidate has REGIME_CHANGE
+2  active mass labor movement and candidate has LABOR or SOCIAL_REVOLUTION
+1  active revolutionary war and candidate has MILITARY_REVOLUTION
```

Candidates with score >= 4 are eligible.

Recruitment UI offers up to the three highest-scoring eligible candidates. Ties are broken deterministically by:
1. higher primary-tag match;
2. higher regional/civilizational affinity;
3. earlier historical activity start;
4. stable internal candidate ID.

The player chooses one of the offered candidates.

AI chooses based on candidate ability fit with its current crisis and strategy.

If no candidate reaches score 4, regional affinity is treated as a soft preference rather than a hard barrier and the system searches globally for a broad-tag compatible candidate. If no globally unrecruited compatible candidate exists at all, GReP remains stored until one becomes eligible. No generic unnamed Great Revolutionary replaces the historical named-person system.

## 10. Worldwide uniqueness

A named Great Revolutionary may be recruited only once worldwide.

Once recruited, that individual is permanently removed from every other political unit's candidate pool.

This preserves the project's global historical-person identity rule.

## 11. Great Philosopher interaction

Great Philosopher and Great Revolutionary remain separate classes.

Political and social philosophers normally stay in Great Philosopher when their representative achievement is theory rather than direct revolutionary leadership.

Great Philosopher abilities may instead:
- grant one-time GReP;
- enable Movement Capacity;
- add a specific candidate tag to the current political context;
- increase GReP from a narrow historical event.

Default person-specific Philosopher GReP effects should normally fall in the +5 to +20 range.

Examples to audit later include Locke, Rousseau and Marx. These names are not automatically reclassified as Great Revolutionaries.

## 12. Great Revolutionary gameplay role

Great Revolutionary is not primarily a yield-burst unit.

The class should change political rules or the cost of political transformation.

Four ability families remain:

### A. Regime-change revolutionary
Typical effects may include:
- switch to an already-unlocked government with greatly reduced or zero Culture switching cost;
- free policy reorganization;
- reduce transition duration;
- reduce, but normally not eliminate, the State Stability shock.

Default V2 template when an individual ability explicitly uses the standard regime-change action:

```text
Government switch Culture cost: -100%
Transition duration: x0.50, minimum 1 turn if a transition would otherwise exist
Government-switch Stability shock: x0.50
Policy reorganization: free once
```

The State Stability numeric layer remains responsible for calculating the underlying shock before the multiplier is applied.

### B. Independence / anti-colonial revolutionary
Typical effects may include:
- unlock an immediate independence decision;
- permit peaceful independence negotiation or independence declaration;
- reduce political transition penalties during independence;
- increase local-unit loyalty or conversion during an independence war when historically appropriate;
- reduce the initial post-independence Stability shock.

Default V2 template:

```text
Independence decision: immediately available
Independence political-transition duration: x0.50
Initial independence Stability shock: x0.50
Free post-independence policy reorganization: 1
```

Military-unit conversion is NOT a universal effect and must be candidate-specific.

### C. Social revolutionary
Typical effects may include:
- immediate progress toward a relevant social civic;
- free adoption/replacement of one relevant social policy;
- temporary reduction of a specific Happiness/Needs burden;
- labor, land, suffrage, citizenship or class-system reform.

No universal flat Science/Culture package is required.

### D. Revolutionary internationalist
Foreign political effects are candidate-specific only.

A Great Revolutionary is not a generic unit that walks into another civilization and presses a Revolution button.

Foreign intervention requires:
- candidate historical justification;
- an existing target-country revolutionary condition;
- explicit individual ability text.

Spy remains the covert foreign-political tool. Great Revolutionary represents overt organized political transformation.

## 13. Map-unit rule

Default Great Revolutionary is a political-person entity attached to a political unit, not a standard roaming civilian unit.

Exceptions:
- candidates historically defined by armed revolutionary leadership may temporarily appear as a leader-type map entity during an independence/revolutionary war;
- these exceptions are candidate-specific and do not turn the class into a Great General substitute.

## 14. Consumption / retirement

Default candidate model:
- one major unique action or transformation package;
- after resolution, the candidate retires and the 15-turn recruitment cooldown begins.

Exceptions may use:
- 2 charges;
- a temporary 10-20 turn movement leader state;
- active leadership through the duration of a revolutionary war.

These exceptions must be historically justified and individually specified.

## 15. Anti-exploit rules

The following are explicitly prohibited:
- GReP from low Happiness alone;
- GReP from ordinary voluntary government switching;
- repeatedly toggling the same civic/policy to regain milestone GReP;
- resetting GReP or recruitment count by colonial independence;
- recruiting multiple unresolved Great Revolutionaries in the same political unit;
- exceeding the GReP cap through stored event overflow;
- recruiting the same historical person in multiple civilizations.

Milestone flags are persistent per political unit or successor-state lineage.

## 16. AI

AI candidate recruitment evaluates:
- current crisis type;
- candidate context score;
- expected government/independence/social-reform utility;
- war state;
- colonial relation;
- City Happiness;
- War Weariness;
- State Stability when quantitatively implemented.

AI does not recruit solely because the threshold is reached if every offered candidate has negligible relevance to the current context. It may hold GReP until context changes.

## 17. UI

Political overview displays:
- current GReP / current threshold;
- Movement Capacity active/inactive;
- current structural-pressure grievances;
- active recruitment cooldown;
- current unresolved Great Revolutionary, if any.

When threshold is reached, the recruitment panel displays up to three eligible candidates and explains why each is eligible through visible tags and context matches.

Colonial-government UI displays its own GReP separately from the metropole.

## 18. V2 locked decisions

Locked:
- Great Revolutionary is event/political-condition based, not specialist based.
- separate GReP pool per political unit.
- separate colonial-government GReP.
- recurring GReP requires Movement Capacity plus grievance.
- 5-turn structural-pressure evaluation interval.
- recurring tick values 4 / 6 / 8 GReP.
- recruitment cost progression 100 / 120 / 140 / 160 / 180 cap.
- overflow retained.
- pool cap = 2x current threshold.
- one unresolved Revolutionary per political unit.
- 15-turn post-resolution recruitment cooldown.
- successor states inherit pool/count/cooldown.
- context-filtered historical candidate pool rather than one unconditional queue.
- up to three highest-scoring candidates offered.
- worldwide historical-person uniqueness.
- no generic unnamed Great Revolutionary fallback.
- default political-person entity, not standard roaming unit.
- Great Philosopher may feed GReP but remains a separate class.
