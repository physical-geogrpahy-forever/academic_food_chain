## Selection rule for this pass

This file is intentionally stricter than a generic rebellion-leader list.

A candidate should normally show all or most of the following:
1. an explicit alternative political or social order, not only resistance to a tax, ruler or occupying army;
2. organized political mobilization, revolutionary society, popular association, constitutional movement or comparable practical activity;
3. direct involvement in attempting to alter the governing or social system;
4. enough historical individuality to support a named Great Person ability.

Simple rebels, dynastic claimants and purely military insurgent commanders are excluded unless the political-program component is substantial.

Cross-class flags do not remove a person yet. They mark later de-duplication against Great Philosopher, Great Writer, Great General and civilization leaders.

## Explicit non-candidates in this pass

The following types are not added solely because they led a rebellion:
- tax-revolt leaders without a durable political or social replacement program;
- peasant or slave revolt leaders whose surviving evidence does not support a distinct constitutional/social program;
- dynastic rebels seeking the throne without a different political order;
- military commanders whose representative achievement is battlefield leadership rather than political organization.

This rule is why the Enlightenment pool is not automatically expanded with every major Atlantic-world uprising.
