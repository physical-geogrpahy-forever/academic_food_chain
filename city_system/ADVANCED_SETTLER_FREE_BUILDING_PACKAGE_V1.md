# Advanced Settler Free Building Package V1

Date: 2026-09-21  
Status: **LOCKED V1**

This file defines which city buildings can be created for free when an advanced founding unit establishes a new city.

## Core rule

Advanced founding units represent a city founded with the ordinary infrastructure of the civilization's current historical stage.

The package is **not** a technology bypass.

A building is granted only when, at the exact moment of founding:

1. the civilization has already satisfied the building's normal technology gate;
2. the civilization has already satisfied its normal civic gate;
3. all local city, terrain and resource conditions are satisfied;
4. prerequisite buildings are present or are also being granted by the same founding package.

If a building is not eligible at founding, it is **not reserved for a delayed free grant**. It must later be constructed normally.

One-per-civilization buildings, government-choice buildings, religious specialization buildings, military-training buildings and special branch-choice institutions are excluded from the standard package unless explicitly added later.

## Tier 1: Pioneer

Era: Exploration  
Free-building cutoff: Renaissance

Core package:
- Monument
- Granary
- Library
- Market
- Aqueduct
- Amphitheater
- Arena
- Workshop
- University
- Bank

Conditional:
- Water Mill if its normal local conditions are met
- Harbor and Lighthouse in an eligible coastal city

Purpose:
A Pioneer city starts as a functioning early-modern town rather than an Ancient-era settlement.

## Tier 2: Colonist

Era: Industrial  
Free-building cutoff: Enlightenment

Includes the entire Pioneer package, then adds:
- Opera House
- Public School
- Stock Exchange
- Newspaper Office
- Zoo

Conditional maritime package:
- Harbor
- Lighthouse
- Seaport

Purpose:
A Colonist city begins as a mature pre-industrial / Enlightenment settlement with established education, finance, culture and public amenities.

## Tier 3: Urban Planner

Era: Modern  
Free-building cutoff: Industrial

Includes the entire Colonist package, then adds:
- Factory
- Hospital
- Sewer
- Food Market
- Cold Storage
- Cinema
- Shopping Mall
- Telegraph Office
- Power Plant

Conditional maritime package:
- Harbor
- Lighthouse
- Seaport
- Shipyard

The two most important additions are explicitly:
- **Factory**
- **Hospital**

These are major Industrial-era institutions and therefore belong in a Modern planned-city founding package.

Power Plant still requires its normal technology/civic and infrastructure conditions. A city does not receive an unavailable energy technology or resource merely because it was founded by an Urban Planner.

## Excluded automatic grants

The standard advanced-settler package does not automatically grant:
- Barracks, Armory, Arsenal, Military Academy, Military Base
- Walls, Castle, Bastion Walls, Star Fort
- Shrine, Temple and other religion-dependent specialization
- Government Plaza or government-building choices
- Great Person guilds
- Art Museum / Archaeological Museum branch choice
- Research Lab
- Medical Lab
- Airport
- Broadcast Center
- Steelworks
- Coal Power Plant
- Drydock

These remain specialization choices or later-era investments.

## Population and territory

The building expansion does not change the already locked population and territory rules:

- Settler: population 1
- Pioneer: population 2
- Colonist: population 3
- Urban Planner: population 4

All four use the **same initial territory rule**.

Thus later founding units gain historical infrastructure, not an oversized population or oversized land claim.
