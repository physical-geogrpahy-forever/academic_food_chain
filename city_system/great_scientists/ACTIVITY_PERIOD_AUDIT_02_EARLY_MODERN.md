## Boundary cases retained

- Halley remains Enlightenment because his synthesis and periodic-comet prediction appeared in 1705.
- William Herschel, Lagrange and Laplace remain Enlightenment because their defining discoveries/publications fall before or at the 1799 boundary, despite careers continuing into the 19th century.
