Date: 2026-09-22
Status: FULL 332-CANDIDATE ACTIVITY-PERIOD AUDIT COMPLETE

## Core rule

Great Scientist era placement follows the person's **principal scientific activity window and the historical work represented by the ability**, not the era of the technology used as an implementation gate.

A single early discovery date is not enough when the defining research program clearly spans a boundary.

Examples corrected during the audit:
- J. J. Thomson remains Modern: electron 1897, atomic-structure model 1899–1904, later positive-ray/isotope work.
- Henri Becquerel remains Modern as a boundary case: radioactivity 1896, continued radiation studies through 1900–1903.
- Enrico Fermi moves to Modern: controlled chain reaction 1942.
- Peter Higgs moves to Atomic: mass mechanism 1964.
- Geoffrey Hinton moves from Future to Atomic: Nobel-recognized Boltzmann-machine work 1983–1985.

## Reconciliation rule

When moving a person to the historically correct era:
1. move the person first;
2. re-check the ability's technology/building gate;
3. if the gate is too late, rewrite the ability;
4. do **not** move the person back to a later era merely to preserve an ability drafted around a later technology.

## Future-era rule

Future remains a technology/gameplay era, but there are no historical people whose actual period of activity is Future.

A historical Great Scientist may become recruitable late because a Future technology is required by their gameplay ability, but their historical era label remains Atomic or Information.
