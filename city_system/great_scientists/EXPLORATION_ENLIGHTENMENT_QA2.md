## Boundary policy

Do not move a person solely by birth/death century when their useful gameplay identity depends on a technology at the next era boundary.

Example:
- Coulomb and Galvani remain Industrial candidates for now because the project's Electricity technology is Industrial and their intended gameplay role is electrical science.
- This is a deliberate 'historical activity + gameplay prerequisite' compromise and will be reviewed in final chronological QA.
