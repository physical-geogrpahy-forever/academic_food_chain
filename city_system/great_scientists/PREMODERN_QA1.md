# Great Scientist Premodern QA1

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: APPLIED

## Scope

Ancient through Renaissance, with emphasis on:
- obvious historical-era misplacements
- ability references to unavailable buildings/technologies
- name-swapped duplicate ability patterns
- cross-class ambiguity

## Era interpretation used

Current project chronology is treated as:
- Late Antiquity: approximately 4th–6th centuries
- Early Medieval: 7th–10th centuries
- High Medieval: 11th–13th centuries
- Renaissance: 14th–15th centuries

Classical therefore includes the major Greco-Roman/Han scientific figures of the first three centuries CE.

## Applied moves

### Late Antiquity -> Classical

- Ptolemy
- Galen
- Diophantus
- Liu Hui
- Zhang Zhongjing
- Wang Fan
- Hua Tuo

Reason:
their principal activity belongs to the first through third centuries CE rather than the project's 4th–6th-century Late Antiquity band.

### High Medieval -> Renaissance

- Ibn al-Shatir
- Ibn Khaldun
- Madhava of Sangamagrama

Reason:
their careers are primarily fourteenth century.

## Added Late Antique candidates

- Theon of Alexandria
- Marinus of Neapolis
- John Philoponus
- Anthemius of Tralles
- Aëtius of Amida
- Alexander of Tralles
- Boethius

These are additions to the longlist, not replacements required to maintain a quota.

## Ability corrections

- Galen no longer requires Alchemy/Apothecary after movement to Classical.
- Zhang Zhongjing no longer requires Apothecary.
- Hua Tuo remains a direct healing aura/burst rather than a later medical-building modifier.
- Ptolemy uses Classical Optics/Library/Lighthouse systems.
- Ibn al-Shatir uses Astrolabe/Lighthouse/trade rather than a much later Observatory.
- Ibn Khaldun uses the existing happiness/yield system instead of an invented social-science subsystem.
- Technology gates now distinguish `researchable` from completed technology where needed.

## Boundary cases retained

- Kamal al-Din al-Farisi (1267–1319)
- Guo Shoujing (1231–1316)

Both straddle the 13th/14th-century boundary and remain High Medieval pending a final rule on whether era assignment follows birth, peak work, or majority active career.
