# Great Scientist Final Activity and Effect QA

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: FIRST-PASS CONTENT QA COMPLETE

## Scope

All 332 historical Great Scientist candidates were audited for:
- principal activity period and era placement;
- historical fit of the drafted ability;
- availability of referenced technologies, buildings and systems;
- exact and near-exact effect duplication;
- repeated generic building-yield patterns;
- obvious within-era power outliers.

## Design corrections completed

Major corrections included:
- moving candidates by actual scientific activity rather than by the technology originally attached to their drafted ability;
- rewriting moved candidates so they do not depend on later-era systems;
- reducing repeated `building +Science/+GPP` effects;
- separating mathematical, astronomical, medical, biological, computing, climate and particle-physics play patterns;
- replacing Carl Sagan's excessive fixed Space Race Production 1200 effect with a proportional project effect;
- separating Abu al-Wafa and Bhaskara II, and Frederick Sanger and David Baker, which were the final near-duplicate pairs.
