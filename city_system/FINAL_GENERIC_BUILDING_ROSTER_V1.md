## Major chains

### Science
Library -> University -> Public School -> Research Lab

Supporting:
Paper Workshop, Scriptorium, Observatory, Chemical Laboratory.

### Culture and media
Monument -> Amphitheater -> Opera House -> **Art Museum OR Archaeological Museum** -> Broadcast Center

Film:
Director's Guild / Cinema -> Film Studio

Great Person guilds:
Writers' Guild, Artists' Guild, Musicians' Guild, Director's Guild.

### Military training
Barracks -> Armory -> Military Academy

Parallel:
Stable, Gunsmith, Arsenal.

### City defense
Walls -> Castle -> Bastion Walls -> Star Fort

Late defense:
Military Base, Radar Station.

### Commerce
Market -> Bank -> Stock Exchange

Parallel:
Mint, Caravansary, Customs Office, Shopping Mall.

### Port
Harbor -> Lighthouse -> Seaport
with Shipyard, Drydock and Navigation School as specialized branches.

### Health and food
Granary / Water Mill
Aqueduct -> Sewer / Hospital -> Medical Lab
Food Market -> Cold Storage
Apothecary remains an early medical branch.

### Industry and power
Workshop -> Factory -> Power Plant

Power-source choice:
**Coal Power Plant OR Nuclear Power Plant OR Solar Plant**

Grid Battery Storage is a later electricity-storage upgrade.

### Entertainment
Land branch:
Arena -> Zoo -> Stadium

Coastal branch:
Ferris Wheel -> Aquarium -> Aquatics Center

A city chooses the Arena branch or Ferris Wheel branch; this preserves the Civ6 Entertainment Complex / Water Park exclusivity without map districts.

Circus remains a separate resource-conditional classical building.

### Government
Government Plaza is one-per-civilization.

Tier 1, choose one:
Ancestral Hall / Audience Chamber / Warlord's Throne

Tier 2, choose one:
Foreign Ministry / Grand Master's Chapel / Intelligence Agency

Tier 3, choose one:
National History Museum / Royal Society / War Department

Consulate -> Chancery is the diplomatic-administration chain.
Court is Palace-linked and one-per-civilization.
