# Great Philosopher Historical Institution and Effect Audit V1

Date: 2026-09-23
Status: MAJOR AUDIT PASS COMPLETE

## Audit rules

### 1. Era compatibility is not enough

A technology, civic or building is not acceptable merely because it belongs to the same broad game era as the philosopher.

The condition/effect should also be plausible for the philosopher's principal activity period and intellectual setting.

### 2. Great Person class and effect content remain separate

A Great Philosopher may have strongly scientific, mathematical, medical, economic or religious effects when those reflect the person's real work.

### 3. Thought chains are internal generation logic only

The five thought chains remain valid for Great Philosopher Point generation:
- knowledge
- religion/nature
- culture/media
- government/diplomacy
- commerce/trade

However, individual Great Philosopher abilities should not expose internal labels such as:
- KNOWLEDGE
- RELIGION_NATURE
- CULTURE_MEDIA
- COMMERCE_TRADE
- active thought chains

Individual abilities should instead refer to visible buildings, specialists, yields, trade routes, government systems or other player-facing mechanics.

### 4. Do not impose Western institutional history globally

University is retained where a university or closely comparable university culture is central to the figure's career.

For figures whose intellectual setting was instead monastic, courtly, private, medical, textual or academy-based, use more appropriate abstractions such as Library, Scriptorium, Temple, Court or Apothecary.

## Major corrections in this pass

### Ancient and Classical

- Thales: replaced internal KNOWLEDGE / RELIGION_NATURE conditions with Library + Shrine/Grove.
- Pythagoras: same internal-chain removal.
- Aristotle: removed Paper Workshop. Library alone now represents the Lyceum/learning context.
- Wang Chong: replaced internal thought-chain labels with Library + Shrine/Grove.

### Late Antiquity

- Proclus and Damascius: replaced active-thought-chain counting with explicit institution counts.
- Sengzhao: replaced KNOWLEDGE with Library.

### Early Medieval

- Wonhyo: replaced three internal thought-chain labels with Temple + Scriptorium + Library.
- Uisang: replaced active-thought-chain counting with Temple + specialist diversity.
- Fazang: replaced active-thought-chain counting with Temple + Scriptorium + Library.
- Dharmakirti: removed Algebra gate. His representative work belongs to sixth-seventh-century Buddhist epistemology and logic; Written Culture is used instead.

### High Medieval

University was removed from figures whose intellectual settings are better represented by monastic, textual, medical or court institutions:
- Ibn Sina
- Anselm of Canterbury
- Maimonides
- Averroes
- Ibn Tufayl
- Suhrawardi
- Zhu Xi
- Madhva
- Fakhr al-Din al-Razi

University remains for figures directly connected to medieval university culture, including:
- Robert Grosseteste
- Peter Abelard
- Thomas Aquinas
- Duns Scotus

### Renaissance

- Ibn Khaldun: replaced COMMERCE_TRADE internal label with Market/Bank.

### Exploration

- Thomas More: replaced COMMERCE_TRADE label with Market/Bank.
- Tommaso Campanella: replaced RELIGION_NATURE with Shrine/Temple/Grove.
- Descartes and Pascal: removed generic University dependency in favor of Library.
- Spinoza and Malebranche: removed generic University dependency.
- Yi Hwang: University replaced with Library for a more appropriate Joseon intellectual setting.
- Margaret Cavendish: University replaced with Printing Press + Library, reflecting her published natural philosophy rather than a university career.
- Anne Conway: University replaced with Library; she received philosophical education privately and by correspondence rather than attending university.

### Enlightenment

- Vico: removed Archaeological Museum; New Science is represented through print/publication and civic-history progress.
- Jeong Je-du: removed Public School; Library + Temple used instead.
- Hong Dae-yong: removed Public School; Observatory + Library used instead.
- Park Ji-won: removed Newspaper Office; Printing Press and trade retained.
- d'Alembert, Berkeley, Diderot, Condillac, Wollstonecraft, d'Holbach, Ogyu Sorai and Mary Astell: Public School dependencies reduced or replaced where mass public-school infrastructure would be too late or too generic.

### Industrial

- Jeong Yak-yong: removed Sewer/Hospital dependency. His ability now focuses on local administration, government institutions and productive reform.
- Choe Han-gi: Telegraph/Telegraph Office removed because he died in 1877 and Korea's first operational telegraph opened in 1885. Scientific Theory + international knowledge exchange retained.
- Comte, Kierkegaard, Bakunin, Nietzsche and Nishi Amane: internal CULTURE_MEDIA labels replaced by explicit buildings.

### Modern

- William James: Research Lab gate removed. Pragmatism is linked to Eureka/Inspiration and religion rather than a late laboratory building.
- Russell, Wittgenstein, Heidegger, Nishida and Jaspers: University-centered conditions now reflect their academic philosophical careers more directly.
- Husserl: Research Lab gate removed in favor of education/university institutions.
- Carnap: Electronics gate removed. Logical Syntax of Language (1934) is a philosophy-of-language/science work, not an electronics milestone.
- Lukacs: CULTURE_MEDIA replaced by explicit media buildings.

### Atomic

- Arendt, Ryle, Deleuze, Rorty and Ham Seok-heon: internal CULTURE_MEDIA / active-thought-chain labels replaced by visible institutions.
- Quine and Ryle: Computers gates removed; representative work is logic, language and philosophy of mind.
- Nagel: Space Race removed. The View from Nowhere concerns objectivity, perspective and ethics, not space exploration.
- Parfit: Computers/Medical Lab linkage removed; Reasons and Persons is represented through civic progress and identity/reasons mechanics.
- Kripke: Computers removed; Naming and Necessity is represented as cross-progress between technology and civics.
- Merleau-Ponty: Medical Lab replaced by Research Lab + University, keeping perception/embodiment without forcing a clinical institution.

### Information

- Butler: Social Media gate removed. Gender Trouble appeared in 1990.
- Charles Taylor: Social Media gate removed.
- Slavoj Zizek: Social Media gate removed; his representative program predates social media.
- David Chalmers: Robotics gate removed. The hard problem was formulated in 1995 and is tied to computation/cognition rather than robotics specifically.
- Bruno Latour: active-thought-chain condition replaced by explicit institutions; Globalization used instead of Environmentalism as the entry gate for the early 1990s actor-network/modernity program.
- Miranda Fricker: Social Media/CULTURE_MEDIA removed; Globalization plus Research Lab/media institutions used instead.
- Nussbaum and Honneth: hidden chain-language replaced by visible institutions.
