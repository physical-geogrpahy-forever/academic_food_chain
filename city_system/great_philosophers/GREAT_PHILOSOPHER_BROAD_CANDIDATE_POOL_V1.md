# Great Philosopher Broad Candidate Pool V1

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: BROAD POOL — NOT YET FINAL ROSTER

## Purpose

This file intentionally builds a broad Great Philosopher candidate pool before pruning.

The class includes:
- metaphysics and epistemology
- ethics
- political philosophy and philosophy of law
- economic philosophy and political economy
- religious philosophy and theology
- social philosophy
- aesthetics
- philosophy of science, language, mind and technology

Class identity and ability content are separate.
A Great Philosopher may therefore have a strongly scientific, mathematical, economic, legal or religious gameplay ability.

## Activity-period rule

Era placement follows principal intellectual activity, using the same boundaries as the Great Scientist project.

This is a **first-pass placement**. Boundary cases must receive a later person-by-person activity audit before the roster is locked.

## Initial design observations

1. The pool is deliberately not quota-balanced by era or region.
2. Western, Chinese, Korean, Indian, Islamic, Jewish and Japanese traditions are all represented where a historically identifiable philosopher can support a distinct gameplay identity.
3. Political philosophy and economic philosophy remain inside Great Philosopher.
4. Theological and religious philosophers are allowed when they are not primarily religion founders.
5. Writer boundary figures remain in the pool for later cross-class review rather than being deleted early.
6. Living or recently active Information-era philosophers may be used because the project already permits historical named figures through the present era.
7. No historical-person Future roster is planned.
