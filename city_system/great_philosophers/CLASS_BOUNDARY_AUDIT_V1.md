# Great Philosopher Class Boundary Audit V1

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: WRITER / PROPHET BOUNDARY PASS COMPLETE

## Rule

Political philosophy, economic philosophy, religious philosophy and social philosophy all belong inside Great Philosopher.

Writing a book, pamphlet, essay, novel or poem does not by itself make someone a Great Writer.

Use Great Writer when literary creation itself is the representative historical achievement.
Use Great Philosopher when the representative achievement is a philosophical, political, economic, ethical, legal or social argument, even when the argument was delivered through highly literary prose.

## Retained as Great Philosopher

### Voltaire
Retain as Philosopher.

Although Voltaire was also a major literary writer, his Enlightenment identity includes a distinctive public conception of philosophy, toleration, critique of fanaticism and defense of natural science. His ability can still use Culture or publishing systems.

### Denis Diderot
Retain as Philosopher.

Diderot was a central Enlightenment philosophe and the Encyclopédie project explicitly aimed to reorganize and transform common ways of thinking. This is a strong Philosopher identity.

### Edmund Burke
Retain as Philosopher.

Burke's representative legacy is political thought, constitutional reasoning and a philosophically informed practical theory of politics. The fact that much of it appeared in speeches and political prose does not make Writer the better class.

### Thomas Paine
Retain as Philosopher.

Paine was a pamphleteer, but his enduring identity rests on republican government, rights, welfare provision, religious criticism and democratic political theory. Political philosophy is explicitly part of the class.

### Park Ji-won (박지원)
Retain as Philosopher.

Park is a major literary figure, but Korean intellectual history also treats him as a central Silhak and Northern Learning thinker. His social criticism, economic thought, practical learning and institutional critique justify Philosopher classification.

His eventual ability should emphasize:
- commerce and material improvement;
- practical learning;
- social criticism;
- Culture/Gold/Science interaction;
rather than simply reproducing a Great Writer work.

### Friedrich Engels
Retain as Philosopher.

Engels belongs to social philosophy, political economy and political theory. Literary form is secondary to the theoretical program.

### Fukuzawa Yukichi
Retain as Philosopher.

His representative identity combines social philosophy, political thought, education and modernization theory. Writer is not required merely because his influence was text-based.

### Iris Murdoch
Retain as Philosopher.

Murdoch was both an important novelist and a professional moral philosopher. Her philosophical identity is independently substantial, especially moral realism, attention, goodness and virtue.

### B. R. Ambedkar
Retain as Philosopher.

Ambedkar's representative identity includes political philosophy, social equality, law, caste critique and democratic constitutional thought. He is not merely a political leader or legal drafter.

## Moved to Great Writer priority

### Rabindranath Tagore
Move to Great Writer priority.

Tagore had a substantial philosophical and educational worldview, but his globally representative historical identity is literary: poet, novelist, dramatist, songwriter and Nobel laureate in literature.

His philosophical ideas can be expressed through a Great Writer ability or Great Work effects rather than occupying a Philosopher slot.

## Prophet boundary

The following figures remain outside the active Philosopher pool because founding or major reform of a religious tradition is the stronger Great Person identity:

- Gautama Buddha
- Jesus
- Muhammad
- Guru Nanak
- Martin Luther
- John Calvin

Religious philosophers who are not primarily founders or institutional reformers remain eligible for Great Philosopher. Examples include:
- Augustine
- Al-Ghazali
- Anselm
- Maimonides
- Averroes
- Thomas Aquinas
- Wonhyo
- Zhu Xi
