# Scientist to Great Philosopher Transfer Audit V1

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: CLASS-IDENTITY AUDIT

## Rule

Class identity and ability content are separate.

A historical figure may move from Great Scientist to Great Philosopher while retaining a strongly scientific, mathematical or medical ability when that effect represents real work.

The transfer question is therefore:
**"What is the person's representative historical identity?"**

not:
**"Does the current ability produce Science?"**

## Current recommendation

Initial Scientist -> Philosopher transfer pool: **18 people**.

Transfer does not imply rewriting every ability into Culture or Faith.
Scientific effects should be retained or even strengthened when historically appropriate.

Particularly important examples:
- Descartes: Philosopher class, but analytic geometry / mathematical method effect.
- Francis Bacon: Philosopher class, but empirical-method / Eureka effect.
- Leibniz: Philosopher class, but calculus / binary / specialist interaction effect.
- Ibn Sina: Philosopher class, but medicine / Health effect can remain.
- Aristotle: Philosopher class, but science/classification/specialist learning can remain.

## Not yet handled

This audit covers only people already present in the Great Scientist roster.

A separate candidate-building pass should add philosophers who were never Scientists, including political philosophers, economic philosophers, theologians, ethicists and philosophers of law.
