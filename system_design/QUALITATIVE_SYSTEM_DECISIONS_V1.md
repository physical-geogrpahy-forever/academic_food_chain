# Qualitative System Decisions V1

Date: 2026-09-21

## Foreign Trade diplomacy
Civ6 Foreign Trade directly enables:
- Joint War
- Join Ongoing War

Both are retained.

## Envoys
Direct Civ6 rewards retained:
- Mysticism -> +1 Envoy
- Military Training -> +1 Envoy

Other already-locked envoy rewards remain where previously audited.

## Global Trade Network
The deferred Telecommunications long-distance commercial-capacity item is merged into:
**Telecommunications + Globalization -> Global Trade Network**

No duplicate subsystem is created.

## Patronage and Great People
Civ5 Patronage's city-state Great Person gift concept is retained in adapted form:
**allied city-states support Great Person generation**

The project does not require random delivery of a whole Great Person.
Exact Great Person Point contribution is numerical balance.

## Labor Disputes
Labor Movement creates a qualitative **Labor Dispute Pressure** system.

Potential triggers:
- poor worker welfare;
- extreme industrial conditions;
- weak labor concessions.

Potential effects:
- Production loss;
- unrest/amenity pressure;
- wage/Gold pressure.

Mitigation:
- Collective Bargaining;
- Factory Acts;
- later welfare/labor policies.

Exact thresholds and magnitudes are numerical/economic balance.

## Result
All non-numerical generic SYSTEM_DESIGN deferrals are resolved.
