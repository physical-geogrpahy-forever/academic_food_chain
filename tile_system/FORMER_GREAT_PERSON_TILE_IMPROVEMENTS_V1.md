# Former Great Person Tile Improvements V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: **LOCKED QUALITATIVE BASELINE**

## Core rule

Former Civilization V Great Person tile improvements are no longer Great-Person-exclusive super improvements.

The project keeps them as **nerfed ordinary Worker-buildable tile improvements**.

A Great Person is **not required and is not consumed**.

Exact yields, build time, terrain restrictions and per-city/per-empire caps remain for the numerical tile-balance pass.

## Included improvements

### Academy
Former source: Great Scientist

Project:
- Worker-built
- Education unlock
- Science-specialized
- Scientific Theory gives a later Academy Science upgrade
- Atomic Theory gives another late Science upgrade
- Great Scientist is not consumed

### Manufactory
Former source: Great Engineer

Project:
- Worker-built
- Manufacturing unlock
- Production-specialized
- Chemistry gives a later Production upgrade
- Great Engineer is not consumed

### Customs House
Former source: Great Merchant

Project:
- Worker-built
- Economics + Mercantilism
- Gold/trade-specialized
- Great Merchant is not consumed

To avoid a duplicate name, the previously adapted city building becomes **Customs Office**.

### Holy Site
Former source: Great Prophet

Project:
- Worker-built
- Theology civic
- Faith-specialized
- requires an established religion in the working city
- Great Prophet is not consumed

This is a tile improvement, not the Civilization VI Holy Site district.

### Landmark
Former source: Great Artist before Brave New World

Project:
- Worker-built
- Humanism civic
- Culture/Tourism-specialized
- Great Artist is not consumed

Natural History retains a separate archaeology pathway:
an Archaeologist may preserve an Antiquity Site as an age/history-based **archaeological Historic Landmark**.

Thus the generic Humanism Landmark and archaeological Historic Landmark are related but distinct mechanics.

## Excluded improvement

### Citadel
Former source: Great General

**Not restored.**

Reasons:
- military tile defense already has the Fort -> Bastion Fort -> Advanced Fortification progression;
- Civ5 Citadel's territorial seizure and adjacent-unit damage are too specialized to become an ordinary Worker improvement;
- Great General remains a command/military Great Person rather than a tile-construction resource.

## Great People after this change

Great People retain non-tile roles.

Examples:
- Great Scientist: research/science action
- Great Engineer: engineering/production action
- Great Merchant: trade mission and corporation founding
- Great Prophet: religion actions
- Great Artist: Great Works / cultural action
- Great General: command and military bonuses
- Great Director: Great Works of Film

None of the five retained former Great Person improvements requires consuming these Great People.

## Source reference

Civilization V former Great Person improvement set:
Academy, Manufactory, Customs House, Holy Site, Landmark, Citadel.

Project result:
**keep the first five in nerfed Worker-buildable form; exclude Citadel.**
