# Government Building Choices V1

Date: 2026-09-21

The project removes the Civilization VI Government Plaza district but preserves its mutually exclusive building-choice system as **one-per-civilization city buildings**.

## Tier 1
Available after adopting Autocracy, Oligarchy, or Classical Republic:
- Ancestral Hall
- Audience Chamber
- Warlord's Throne

Choose one.

## Tier 2
Available after adopting any Tier-2 government such as Monarchy, Theocracy, or Merchant Republic:
- Foreign Ministry
- Grand Master's Chapel
- Intelligence Agency

Choose one.

## Tier 3
Available after adopting Democracy, Fascism, or Communism:
- National History Museum
- Royal Society
- War Department

Choose one.

## Structural rule
- no Government Plaza map district;
- one government-administration center per civilization;
- one mutually exclusive building at each tier;
- source Governor-title effects are deferred if the project does not adopt Governors;
- exact numeric effects are handled in the later government-balance pass.
