## Rules

1. Every person in this file is eligible to appear as a signature Great Merchant or Great Engineer.
2. Recruiting the Great Person does not automatically create the company.
3. The player may:
   - use the Great Person's ordinary ability; or
   - consume the Great Person to establish the signature named firm if its gate is satisfied.
4. The named historical firm is unique globally.
5. The canonical sector is never unique.
6. If the signature firm is not founded, generic corporations in the same sector remain available.
7. A firm's founder-era primary sector is fixed historically; later sectors are entered through the Diversification Project.
8. Liu Chuanzhi appears as **Founding-team leader / co-founder**, not sole founder.
9. Ozires Silva is omitted because Embraer was state-created; he remains in the research database only.
