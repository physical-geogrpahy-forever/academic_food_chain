# Corporate Founder Pool Research V1

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: RESEARCH POOL — broad candidate collection, not every candidate is locked

## Design conclusions from founder research

The corporation system should support a **large founder pool**, not a small Wonder-like list.

A signature founder has:
- Great Person class: Great Merchant or Great Engineer
- signature firm
- sector
- output class
- signature product/service
- historical founder/co-founder note

The named firm is unique, but **the sector is never exclusive**.

## Output classes

### Manufactured Luxury
Consumer-facing branded goods.
Primary effect family:
- Amenities/Happiness
- Gold
- export demand
- optional Culture/Tourism for globally iconic brands

Examples:
Jeans, Cosmetics, Chocolate, Fashion Goods, Furniture, Automobiles, Consumer Electronics, Sporting Goods.

### Functional Corporate Good
A supplied city must actually receive quantities of the product.
Primary effect depends on sector.

Examples:
- Refined Petroleum Products -> Production + transport/logistics
- Steel Products -> Production + construction
- Machine Tools -> Production + factory efficiency
- Fertilizer -> Food + farm productivity
- Pharmaceuticals -> Growth/Health + healing
- Electrical Equipment -> Production + Science
- Precision Components -> Production + advanced-unit production
- Semiconductors -> Science + Production + advanced units
- Tires -> logistics / vehicle efficiency
- Industrial Chemicals -> Production / advanced industry
- Aircraft -> long-range logistics / trade / aviation capacity

### Service
Normally supplied by branches/networks rather than stockpiled as a physical resource.

Examples:
- Finance -> Gold + investment
- Retail -> Gold + product distribution
- Hospitality -> Tourism + Culture + Gold
- Film/Entertainment -> Culture + Tourism + Great Director ecosystem
- Telecommunications -> Science + network/trade efficiency
- Logistics -> Trade route / movement / Gold
- Digital Platforms -> Gold + Science + service reach
- Software -> Science + Production + Gold

Services should **not** all give Culture/Tourism.

## Mechanics implied by the research

### 1. Functional goods should use physical supply quantities

Example:

Oil -> Refinery corporation -> Refined Petroleum Products -> supplied cities

Suggested city effects:
- Production bonus
- logistics/transport efficiency
- lower civilian fuel/logistics burden
- bonus to industrial/transport buildings

A refinery company therefore cares about:
- Oil input
- refinery capacity
- branch/factory capacity
- transport network
- domestic/foreign demand

### 2. Services should use network coverage

Service companies should usually not create a stockpiled resource.

Examples:

**Hilton / Marriott**
- branch = hotel
- Tourism + Culture + Gold
- stronger in cities receiving foreign trade/tourists

**Goldman Sachs / J.P. Morgan**
- branch = financial office
- Gold
- investment pool / cheaper corporate expansion
- Production boost through financing, not Culture

**Aramex**
- branch = logistics office
- internal/foreign trade efficiency
- branch supply range
- movement/logistics bonus

**Ericsson / Bharti / Econet / Celtel**
- branch/network = telecommunications coverage
- Science
- Gold
- corporate/service network range
- later Social Media / digital-company synergy

**Grab / Careem**
- urban mobility service
- Gold
- city connection / local movement efficiency
- possible Tourism/commuter efficiency, but not primarily Culture

### 3. Some products are hybrid

**Automobiles**
- manufactured luxury: Amenities + brand demand
- functional: city logistics / land transport efficiency

**Consumer Electronics**
- manufactured luxury: Amenities
- functional: Science / information access

**Musical Instruments**
- manufactured luxury: Amenities
- cultural: Culture / Great Musician synergy

**Sporting Goods**
- manufactured luxury: Amenities
- cultural/service synergy: Professional Sports

This is preferable to forcing every corporate output into only one yield.

### 4. Same product category should not stack Amenities infinitely

Example:
Ford + Toyota + Honda + Hyundai can all exist.

All may produce the category **Automobiles**, but:
- first Automobile category available to a civilization grants the main Amenity/Happiness effect;
- extra brands add Gold, Tourism, trade demand, market share and corporate competition;
- a signature brand may have a founder-specific bonus.

This preserves corporate competition without making five car companies equal five separate luxury resources.
